# Modern Movie Crew — Product Constitution

## Governing Principle
> *"Many members performing distinct functions, yet contributing to one body."* (1 Corinthians 12)

Modern Movie Crew is a distributed production operating system for generative filmmaking. The platform succeeds not by making every contributor the director, but by honoring each distinct contribution while maintaining creative unity and canonical governance.

## Mission Statement
Generative tools unbundle traditional film production resources—funding, equipment, labor, voice, performance, and editorial judgment. Modern Movie Crew rebundles these scarce resources around individual projects governed by script-driven workflows and clear film constitutions.

## Core Pillars

### 1. Model Agnostic
- The platform does not host internal video/audio generation models or AI writing tools.
- Contributors generate assets using their existing accounts, tools, or workflows (e.g. Sora, Runway, Veo, Kling, Suno, ElevenLabs).
- The platform tracks, contextually governs, and archives provenance for external generations.

### 2. Governed Creative Unity (Film Constitution)
- Every film defines a creative constitution (visual language, camera rules, aspect ratio, color script, lighting, performance tone).
- Higher-level constraints flow down: **Film Constitution → Sequence Rules → Scene Brief → Shot Packet → Submitted Generation**.
- Creative freedom occurs within the shot packet without redefining the film.

### 3. Accountable Human Decisions
- Automation does not replace editorial judgment.
- A task moves forward through explicit, atomic service functions called by authorized human roles.
- Department heads approve production packets; directors/reviewers accept or request revisions on exact versions.

### 4. Ownership & Immutable Provenance
- Clear terms governing license/assignment, likeness permissions, and commercial suitability of tools.
- Granular contribution tracking recorded in an append-only audit trail and credit ledger.
- Screen credits, revenue participation, and voting authority derive directly from verified contributions.
