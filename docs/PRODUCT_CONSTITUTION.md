# Modern Movie Crew — Product Constitution

## Governing Principle
> *"Many members performing distinct functions, yet contributing to one body."* (1 Corinthians 12)

Modern Movie Crew is a distributed production operating system for generative filmmaking. The platform succeeds not by making every contributor the director, but by honoring each distinct contribution while maintaining creative unity and canonical governance.

## Mission Statement
Generative tools unbundle traditional film production resources—funding, equipment, labor, voice, performance, character continuity, set design, and editorial judgment. Modern Movie Crew rebundles these resources around individual projects governed by script-driven workflows and clear film constitutions.

## Product Boundary
Modern Movie Crew strictly organizes:
- Projects & Structure (Acts, Sequences, Scenes, Tasks)
- Project-Scoped Character Library (Identity, Looks, Voice Profiles, Performance Profiles, Scene States, Rights Records)
- Departments & Roles
- Production Resources & Reference Packets
- Task Claims & Submissions
- Immutable Versions & Two-Layer Reviews
- Append-Only Canonical Selections
- Provenance & Contribution Credit Ledgers

**Out of Scope**: The platform is NOT a payment, tokenized investment, crypto/financial exchange, or automatic ownership distribution platform. It generates structured contribution reports and credit exports that external production systems or contracts may reference.

## Core Pillars

### 1. External Model Agnostic
- The platform does not host internal generation models or transfer AI generation credits/accounts.
- Contributors use their own external tools and accounts (e.g. Sora, Runway, Veo, Kling, Suno, ElevenLabs) and bring generated assets into the governed project.

### 2. Governed Creative Unity & Character Library
- Every film defines a creative constitution and a canonical **Character Library**.
- Permanent character identity (`CharacterIdentityVersion`) is strictly separated from scene-specific appearance (`CharacterLook`, `CharacterSceneState`).
- Constraints flow down: **Film Constitution → Character Library → Sequence Rules → Scene Brief → Task Packet → Submitted Generation**.

### 3. Accountable Human Decisions & Two-Layer Review
- Automation does not replace editorial judgment.
- Department heads (Art, Costume, Sound, Performance, Continuity) review work (`APPROVED`, `ISSUE_FOUND`, `REVISION_RECOMMENDED`) to ensure departmental consistency.
- Authorized directors make final creative decisions (`ACCEPT`, `ACCEPT_AS_ALTERNATE`, `REQUEST_REVISION`, `REJECT`).

### 4. Immutable Provenance & Legal Attestations
- Character likeness permissions, voice rights, and actor licensing are tracked in `CharacterRightsRecord`.
- Contributors complete `SubmissionAttestation` confirming authority, external tools, commercial status, and likeness permissions.
- Revisions are immutable; canonical selections are append-only.
- Comprehensive credit ledger derived from verified responsibility, character design, costume styling, voice direction, department reviews, and accepted footage.
